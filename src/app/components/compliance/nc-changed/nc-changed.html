Two federal changes. Eighteen months apart. Pulling in opposite directions. Then add your state.

Washington's My Health My Data Act. Nevada SB 370, effective January 2026. Minnesota's consumer health data provisions. California AB 45, restricting geofencing around health facilities. New York's proposed health information privacy act. None of these are HIPAA, and all of them can reach a practice that treats HIPAA as the finish line.
