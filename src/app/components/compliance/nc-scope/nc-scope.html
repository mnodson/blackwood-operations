What this is, and what it is not

We are direct about scope, because the alternative is a client who thinks they bought something they did not.

This is a service that keeps a specific public-facing document current, drafted for your practice, monitored against federal and state change, and delivered in usable formats.

This is not legal advice, and Blackwood Operations is not a law firm. Your notice should be reviewed by your own counsel before you adopt it. Most of our clients find that review takes minutes rather than hours, because the drafting is already done and the reasoning behind each change is explained.

This is also not a full HIPAA compliance program. Your notice is one required element. Risk analysis, policies and procedures, workforce training, and business associate agreements are separate obligations. We will tell you plainly when something falls outside this service. If you want help with the rest, that is a different conversation and we are happy to have it.
