The problem with your notice

Maybe it's on your website. Maybe it's printed on a piece of paper that your receptionist hands to patients when they arrive.

Your Notice of Privacy Practices is the only compliance document your patients, your state board, and the Office for Civil Rights can all read without asking your permission. It is posted in your lobby. It is on your website. It is handed to every new patient.

It is also, for most practices, the least maintained document in the building.
