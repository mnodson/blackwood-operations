This policy covers blackwoodops.com and the services we deliver through it. It is written in plain language on purpose — we sell notice clarity, so our own notice should be clear.

What we collect

We only collect information you hand us, plus the ordinary technical records a website keeps.

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, company, and the message you write. If you arrive from our free assessment, the answers and score you chose to send with it come too.
  • Compliance signup. Practice name, contact name, email, phone, practice type, number of providers and locations, the states you practice in, whether you currently have a Notice of Privacy Practices, your plan selection, and any notice document you upload.
  • Scheduling. If you book a call through the embedded Calendly widget, Calendly collects your name, email, and the time you choose, under its own policy.
  • Technical records. Standard server logs from our host — IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps.

We do not collect card details. Payment is completed on Stripe's hosted checkout and your card number never reaches our systems.

How we use it

To answer you, to prepare and maintain the documents you purchase, to bill you, and to tell you when a regulatory change affects your notice. That last one is the product.

We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that make the business run — our email and hosting providers, Calendly for scheduling, and Stripe for payment — and only with what each one needs. We will also disclose information where the law requires it.

Documents you upload

A notice you upload during signup is a public-facing document, and we treat it as confidential business material regardless. We use it to understand your current position and to draft your replacement notice. Please do not upload patient records or any protected health information — we do not need it, and our signup form is not the place for it.

Cookies and third-party embeds

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The Calendly scheduling widget is loaded from Calendly's servers and sets its own cookies when you interact with it; if you would rather avoid that, email us and we will book a time by hand.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while we are in conversation and for a reasonable period afterwards. Customer records and the documents we issue are kept while we are working for you, including for as long as any monitoring continues, and then for as long as we need them to evidence what we delivered and when. Ask us to delete your information and we will, except where we are required to retain it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live — California, Washington, Nevada, Colorado and others — you may have specific statutory rights, including rights over consumer health data. We honour these requests from everyone, not only residents of those states. Email us and we will action it.

Security

Information is held in access-controlled accounts with reputable providers, and access is limited to the people who need it to do the work. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects your information we will tell you promptly and tell you what we are doing about it.

Contact us

Email admin@blackwoodops.com with any privacy question or request. If a change to this policy materially affects how we handle your information, we will update the date at the top and say what changed.
